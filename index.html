Sources & how to read this

Covered in this version: grades 3 to 5, math and ELA, for North Carolina, Michigan and Texas. Standard codes follow each state's published numbering (NC Standard Course of Study, Michigan/CCSS cluster codes, TEKS student expectations). Pacing orders are representative examples, since none of these states mandates pacing. Confirm codes and order against your district's current documents before planning.

Scaffold steps move from concrete to representational to abstract and are suggestions for planning, intervention and small groups.
